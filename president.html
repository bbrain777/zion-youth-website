Olakunle T. Obademi

President of ZION Youth Development Initiative, educator, and emerging technology professional.

Leadership Profile

Olakunle T. Obademi is a visionary leader, educator, and emerging technology professional dedicated to empowering communities through education, capacity building, and sustainable development. As the President of ZION Youth Development Initiative since 2022, he has spearheaded programs that provide youth and marginalized communities across Africa with access to quality education, vocational skills, and global opportunities.

With a strong background in science and technology, Olakunle earned his B.Sc. in Physics & Electronics from the Federal University of Technology, Akure, Nigeria, and is currently pursuing a Software Development degree at Brigham Young University (BYU). His academic foundation is complemented by hands-on experience in computer literacy training, IT hardware assembly, and network setup.

From 2012 to 2018, Olakunle served as a Sciences and Computer Studies Teacher at DUZEK Christian School in Johannesburg, South Africa, where he taught mathematics, physical sciences, and computer literacy. Later, as a Computer Training Tutor at Obamarg Global in Nigeria, he mentored learners in digital skills and equipped them for modern workforce demands.

Focus Areas

Education access, practical technology training, mentorship, and international opportunity pathways for young people.

Community Projects & Activities

Since founding ZION Youth Development Initiative, Olakunle has led grassroots and youth empowerment projects with a strong focus on Ondo State, Nigeria, while expanding outreach to other communities.

Youth IT Skills Training - Akure, Ondo State

Organized workshops teaching computer literacy, coding fundamentals, and digital job readiness.

School Support Project - Owo, Ondo State

Partnered with local leaders to support learning spaces and provide educational resources.

Community Outreach - Ifedore

Coordinated volunteers to provide practical support and connect families with helpful resources.

Scholarship & Mentorship Program

Connected talented African youth with scholarship opportunities and application guidance.
